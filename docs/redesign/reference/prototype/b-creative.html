~/elisa/about

Hi, I'm Elisa. I like problems.

I started in 2011 writing PHP portal systems. Somewhere along the way I became the person teams call when a migration stalls, a site gets hacked, or a checkout breaks at 2am.

I've built for clients in Australia, the US, the UK and South Africa, and I still enjoy the moment a stubborn bug finally makes sense.

  1. Find the real problem first.

    I'd rather spend an hour diagnosing than a week fixing the wrong thing.

  2. Leave it better documented than I found it.

    Handovers, redirect maps, and notes for whoever comes next.

  3. Say the uncomfortable thing early.

    If the timeline, plugin or plan won't work, you'll hear it in week one, not week six.

I've also led the team.

Scrum, client reporting and dev coordination at 2nd Office. Later, UX and UI teams as a senior engineer. Today I coordinate SEO specialists, ads, copywriters and junior developers on client projects.
