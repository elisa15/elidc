One beauty brand, four storefronts.

For two years I looked after a US beauty brand's Shopify store, then replicated it for Australia, Europe and Canada, with filtering and colour swatches written in custom code instead of bolted on with apps.

The situation

The brand sells beauty products online, with a catalogue of more than 1,000 SKUs. The US store was the original. The brand wanted the same shopping experience in Australia, Europe and Canada.

What made it tricky

Colour is the product, so shoppers need to filter and compare shades fast. The obvious route was stacking apps for filtering and swatches. On a catalogue this size, every app adds load time, cost and another thing that can break, four times over.
